Model Connection Instructions

Follow these steps to connect your Python model backend:

  1. Copy your model folder into backend/src/atlas_rag/.
  2. Open backend/src/atlas_rag/live_rag.py and call your model functions inside query() and ingest_document().
  3. Your model is now fully connected to the application.
